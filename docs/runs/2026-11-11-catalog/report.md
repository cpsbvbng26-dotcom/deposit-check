## What to do

- **`abstract-missing`** Add the abstract as a description of the type Abstract at the repository. Indexes such as OpenAlex and Google Scholar read it from there; the PDF alone is not enough.
- **`abstract-short`** Give the abstract of the work itself, not a one-line summary or the title again.
- **`licence-missing`** Choose a licence at the repository (for example CC BY 4.0). Without one, readers may not reuse the work, and some indexes do not list it as open.
- **`keywords-missing`** Add a few keywords (subjects) at the repository. They help indexes and readers find the work.
- **`language-missing`** Give the language of the work (for example en or ja).
- **`relations-none`** Where they exist, link the code, the data, other copies of the work and its published version (for example IsSupplementedBy, IsIdenticalTo, IsPreprintOf).
- **`openalex-abstract-missing`** OpenAlex reads the abstract from the DOI metadata when it next updates the work. If it still has none after some weeks, report it to OpenAlex through its support form.
- **`not-on-orcid`** Add it to the ORCID record: Works, Add works, Search & link, DataCite or Crossref. Or let them update the record for you (auto-update).
